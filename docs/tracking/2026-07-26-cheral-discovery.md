# Discovery: Cheral — Traveling Historical Trust

**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Lead:** Cam (Coach Cam)
**Status:** Complete
**Prior Phase:** None

## Vision & Purpose
Cheral is a modern, elegant web platform for historical travel, ancient heritage exploration, and cultural trust. It presents rich destination stories, historical landmarks, guided itineraries, and trust verification for heritage travel.

## Key Decisions
- Chose Warm Off-White (`#F7F3EF`) as primary page background to create a warm minimalist, magazine-style layout instead of stark white.
- Chose Charcoal Black (`#222222`) over pure black for typography to reduce contrast strain and enhance editorial elegance.
- Chose Coral Orange (`#F68B5F`) and Soft Terracotta (`#E26E4D`) as primary interactive accents (10% ratio) for CTAs, highlights, and micro-interactions.
- Adopted dual bilingual logo assets: English logo (`logos/Cheral eng logo copy.png`) and Tamil transparent logo (`logos/Final Cheral logo transparent.png`), with `logos/Final Cheral logo copy.png` serving as favicon.
- Adopted 70% Neutral / 20% High-Res Photography / 10% Accent color proportion for a Scandinavian-inspired UI with Mediterranean warmth.
