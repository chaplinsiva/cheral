## What Problem Does It Solve?

- **Bridging Nature & Heritage:** Connects ecological biodiversity conservation with historical consciousness in South India.
- **Community Engagement:** Facilitates student education, guided heritage walks, native tree plantations, and public awareness campaigns.
- **Trust & Transparency:** Provides clear organization credentials, bank details (South Indian Bank), UPI payment ID, and transparent accountability for donors and volunteers.
- **Bilingual Accessibility:** Supports both English and Tamil (`ta`) for local community and global historical trust engagement.
